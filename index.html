Privacy & security

The current development integration is designed around limited access and local processing.

Services only
The integration requests only the Planning Center Services OAuth scope (services). It does not request access to other Planning Center products.
Read-only behavior
The app enforces GET-only access to permitted Services resources. It does not create, edit, or delete Planning Center data. This restriction is enforced by the app; the Services scope itself is not a read-only permission. OAuth token exchange is separate from resource access.
Temporary authorization
Sign-in happens with Planning Center through OAuth with PKCE. The app does not collect your Planning Center password or use a client secret. Authorization codes and tokens are handled transiently by the local OAuth helper; they are not intentionally persisted to disk, included in saved plan exports, or exposed by this webpage.
You choose what to save
Selected plan information is processed locally. Saving a plan export requires a separate confirmation in the local tool. That export contains plan information, not OAuth credentials.

This informational page has no sign-in, forms, analytics, cookies, or third-party scripts. It does not connect to Planning Center or receive your plan data or tokens. The hosting provider may process standard request logs when you visit.
